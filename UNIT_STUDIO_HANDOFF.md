# 3D Unit & Building Design Studio — Main Agent Integration Handoff (All 93 Deep-Engineered Assets)

## 1. Overview

All **93 battlefield assets** across **USA**, **China**, **GLA**, and **Neutral Tech** — comprising **58 Deep-Engineered Units** (`ZH_DATA.UNITS` + 11 Sub-General & Variant IDs) and **35 Deep-Engineered Buildings** (`ZH_DATA.BUILDINGS` + 4 Neutral Tech Buildings finalized in `Ally Building List`) — are now **Deep-Engineered** in [`js/unit_models.js`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/js/unit_models.js). Every unit and building features:
- **5-Group Parametric Inspector Schema** (`paramSchema` with `groups[].fields[]`) in [`js/unit_models.js`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/js/unit_models.js#L5028-L7191)
- **5 Tactical & Architectural Presets** (`presets`, including `DIORAMA_DEFAULT` and 4 doctrine/combat/fortification variants)
- **Dedicated Multi-Pass 3D Ligne-Claire Procedural Renderer** (`render`) with Painter's-algorithm depth sorting, 360° turntable rotation, independent 3D turret/launcher/radar/superweapon articulation, and live recoil/suspension/hydraulic/superweapon kinematics.
- **Full `DEEP_ENGINEERED_UNIT_IDS` Registration**: [`DEEP_ENGINEERED_UNIT_IDS`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/js/unit_models.js#L9618) is populated with `Object.keys(MODEL_REGISTRY)` (all 93 IDs) so every unit and building renders with its full 3D Ligne-Claire rig in both [`unit_studio.html`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/unit_studio.html) and the live game ([`index.html`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/index.html) via [`js/vfx.js`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/js/vfx.js#L253-L259) and [`js/vfx.js#L1062-L1110`](file:///Users/muhammad.shan/Documents/cnc-generals-zero-hour-mac/js/vfx.js#L1062-L1110)).
